Game Plan:

    High Level:
    - A. Have autonomous gens (game name for people {french for folks})
    - B. Have complex companies, ran by gens, that can specialize in a variety of industries
    - C. Have raw products that must be extracted or farmed. For now, every raw product will only be used by factories to make secondary products.
    - D. Factories use raw products to make secondart products.
    - E. Secondary products are used by compaies to enable or aid production, extraction, farming, or transport.
    - F. Game  Events are what drives the Gen's and company's actions. Each entity has a set number of actions each day which can be used in response to an event, or used to create an event.


    Detailed:

    A - Gens
        Variables:
        > String name, int age, Traits traits, Inventory items

        Req. Classes:
        > Traits - Stores and modifies the traits & stats of the gen 
            - 2D array struct
            - 
        > Inventory - Stores the items & money the gen has
            - 2D Array
        > Brain - Stores memories that affect decisions, makes decisions based of internal & external factories
            - Brain needs to decide which action is the best for any given situation.

    B - Companies

    C - Raw Products

    D - Factories

    E - Secondary Products

    F - Game Events
